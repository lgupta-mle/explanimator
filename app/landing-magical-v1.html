The same six steps a great teacher
would take, taken for you.

Six volumes, bound. Each chapter cached, so re-runs are quiet and quick. Tweak the difficulty, the voice, the running example — the rest follows.

I · EXTRACTIO
A quiet reading.
We parse the table of contents, find the chapter boundaries, set aside the references, the appendices, the exercises. What remains is the spine of the work.
II · DIGESTIO
A concept map, drawn.
Every definition, every equation, every worked example finds its place on a graph — a private constellation of ideas the lecture will trace.
III · EXPLICATIO
A script, written.
A scene-by-scene plan: the cold open, the analogy, the derivation, the worked example. Held together by a Series Bible — same notation, same voice.
IV · FIGURÆ
The figures, generated.
Manim code, written by a careful hand. A validator catches overlapping shapes and orphaned objects. The figures move only as much as they must.
V · VOX
A voice, given.
A narrator reads each beat aloud, paced to the figures. Three registers to choose: warm and curious, crisp and confident, low and even.
VI · OPUS
The lecture, delivered.
FFmpeg muxes audio and video into a single MP4. Saved to your library, with a transcript, chapter marks, and a permanent timestamp.
